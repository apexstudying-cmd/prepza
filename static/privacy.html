Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Prepza collects, why, and how it's handled, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Academic information: your year and semester, used to show you relevant content.
  • Payment information: M-Pesa transaction records (amount, phone number, transaction ID) processed via Safaricom's Daraja API when you unlock paid content.
  • Usage information: basic session data (e.g. login sessions, which expire after 7 days) and error logs if something goes wrong on the platform.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your email.
  • To grant access to the content tier you've paid for.
  • To process M-Pesa payments and maintain a record of your payment history.
  • To watermark Q&A PDFs with your account email, which protects the content from unauthorized sharing and is a condition of accessing that material.
  • To fix bugs and improve the platform (via error monitoring).
  • To contact you about your account or respond to support requests.

3. Who we share information with

We don't sell your personal information. We do share necessary information with the service providers ("processors") that help Prepza run:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase and secure storage of account data and paid content
Safaricom Daraja APIProcessing M-Pesa payments
BrevoSending account emails (verification, password reset)
SentryError monitoring, to help us detect and fix bugs

Some of these providers may process data on servers located outside Kenya. Each provider is contractually restricted to using your data only to provide their service to Prepza.

4. Cookies & sessions

Prepza uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. Sessions expire automatically after 7 days, after which you'll need to log in again. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and payment information for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations after that.

6. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@prepza.app.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including password hashing, time-limited signed URLs for accessing paid content, and secure, watermarked delivery of sensitive Q&A material. No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep yours as safe as possible.

8. Children's privacy

Prepza is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to support@prepza.app.
